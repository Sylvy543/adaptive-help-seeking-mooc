Online Resource 4 — Feature decomposition under strict accuracy
Revision package: 8 October 2026

The three summary CSV files use the strict student-level outcome and the final feature grouping described in OR1. N = 16,389. The two additional categories, Self-Regulation and Self, are retained alongside KG Navigation, Other Process and Task features to match the original supplementary scope. The folder name refers to the central three-category decomposition, not the omission of the other recorded categories.

three_category_correlations.csv — Five category-share correlations with strict accuracy, including mean/SD of student shares, Pearson/Fisher-z 95% intervals, Spearman coefficients and unadjusted p values. No Spearman confidence interval is supplied here. The selected tests' corrected p/q and bootstrap intervals are in OR7; the five rows here are not a separate BH family.
three_category_tertiles.csv — Descriptive results in fixed strict-accuracy groups: Low <67.5% (n=5,540); Medium 67.5–84.0% inclusive (n=5,467); High >84.0% (n=5,382). Means are equal-student means, not pooled interaction shares. Other is shown separately to reconcile the shares to 100%.
three_category_ttests.csv — Low minus High comparisons, using two-sided Welch tests and Welch 95% confidence intervals for the mean difference. Differences and SDs are in percentage points. Hedges g uses the sample-size-weighted pooled SD, the small-sample correction and an approximate analytic interval. The groups are defined by accuracy; the comparisons are descriptive, not causal. OR7's archived selected-comparison table uses the opposite High minus Low direction, clearly labelled there.
three_category_student_data.csv — The complete per-student feature breakdown referenced in Section 4.7.2: student identifier, strict accuracy, total interactions, category counts/shares and fixed accuracy group. Each share equals its corresponding count divided by ai_frequency, multiplied by 100. kg_count and other_process_count separate KG from MOOCCubeX/Aminer interactions; the other four count groups are Task, Self-Regulation, Self and unclassified Other. Counts sum to ai_frequency; the six shares sum to 100. The four Baike variants follow the neutral mapping documented in OR1. This is not the complete coder-anchored sensitivity mapping.

All p values in the three summary tables are unadjusted. Empty values are not zero. Percentages are stored on a 0–100 scale, not a 0–1 scale. File precision is retained for reproduction.
